Microcredential #2 · Understand the Community

Community Asset Map

A fieldwork organizer for mapping assets, tracing trust, identifying gaps, and applying the Vision Grid and equity lens.

1

Scan the full asset landscape

Name formal and informal assets. Include people, groups, institutions, businesses, networks, spaces, and natural gathering places.

Rubric · Mapping the Assets

Formal organizations

Schools, nonprofits, agencies, health providers, libraries, faith communities, cultural institutions

Informal networks & wisdom

Elders, healers, mutual aid, parent connectors, neighborhood leaders, cultural knowledge keepers

Businesses & resources

Local employers, shops, service providers, funders, transportation, food and technology resources

Youth & gathering spaces

Youth groups, parks, recreation, community rooms, trusted online spaces, and natural gathering places

2

Build the asset inventory

Record what each asset contributes, who trusts it, and the Vision Grid pillar it most naturally serves.

5

Apply the equity lens

Center underserved populations, language access, accessibility, and rural/low-income context throughout—not as an add-on.

Rubric · Vision Grid & Equity

Whose assets or knowledge may be overlooked?

Consider power, language, disability, geography, income, race/ethnicity, immigration status, age, and historic exclusion.

How will community members shape and validate the map?

Identify who will review it, how participation will be accessible, and how community knowledge will be credited.

6

Rubric alignment & evidence check

Use the rubric language to assess the strength of your final map and identify the next revision.

Assessment Readiness

Emerging

  • A few obvious, formal organizations
  • Few or no disconnected assets named
  • Limited pillar or equity connection

Maturing

  • Formal and informal assets across many types
  • Five or more disconnected assets
  • Assets read against all four pillars

Transforming

  • Full landscape plus trust and connection flows
  • Root causes and trust-building moves surfaced
  • Pillars and equity centered throughout
